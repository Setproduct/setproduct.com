A white label SEO platform lets an agency resell SEO services under its own brand, through a dashboard the client never sees the vendor inside. Reach for a full platform when you need audits and reporting in one place, plus link building. Reach for a reseller program when you mainly want to rebrand and resell. Six options compared below.

## TL;DR

→ A platform gives you the full stack under your own logo: audits and reporting, plus on-page work and link building.

→ A reseller program gives you branding and reselling, which suits agencies that already deliver some services in house.

→ Match the tool to the margin you want, because per-client pricing decides whether a deal actually works.

→ Link building is the service most agencies outsource first, so weight each platform's link offering heavily.

## What a white label SEO platform actually is

A white label SEO platform handles delivery behind the scenes and lets your agency put its own name on everything the client sees. You keep the relationship and the margin, while the vendor supplies the tools, the reporting, and often the people doing the work.

That model suits agencies that want to sell SEO without building an in-house team first. The trade-off is dependence: once your reporting, your links, and your dashboards all route through one vendor, switching costs real time and client trust.

## The six platforms, briefly

### AgencyPlatform

AgencyPlatform leans on breadth and support. It bundles on-page and off-page work with content and reporting in a single dashboard, with account managers on call. It reads as the most complete option, and the least transparent on price.

### SEOReseller

SEOReseller puts the emphasis on branding. Agencies can rebrand the service under their own name and ride its toolkit, with solid project management. Support is a step behind AgencyPlatform, and the tooling is not as deep.

### FatJoe

FatJoe is known for backlinks and content. It scales link-building campaigns and outreach, plus press releases, which makes it a good fit for agencies that sell links as a product. Full-service SEO is not its focus.

### Stanventures

Stanventures keeps things simple and link-focused. It delivers quality backlinks without a heavy tool suite, so it works for agencies that only want to resell link building. Response times can lag.

### Semify

Semify targets agencies that want a broad, budget-friendly suite: audits, on-page SEO, and backlink building. It is a value pick rather than a premium one, and the interface shows it.

### StellarSEO

StellarSEO specializes in link building and audits. Its tools cover those two jobs well and little else, so it fits agencies selling focused services rather than a full SEO retainer.

## Who each option fits

→ Agencies selling full retained SEO with no delivery team: AgencyPlatform or Semify.

→ Agencies that want to rebrand and keep delivery in house: SEOReseller.

→ Agencies selling link building as a standalone product: FatJoe or Stanventures, and StellarSEO for the narrowest fit.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a white label SEO platform?

It is a service that delivers SEO work behind the scenes while your agency puts its own brand on the dashboards and reports, plus communication. Clients deal with you, not the vendor. You set the price and keep the difference between what you charge and what the platform charges you.

### What is the difference between a white label SEO platform and a reseller program?

A platform usually supplies tools and delivery, plus reporting across many services, while a reseller program focuses on letting you rebrand and resell, sometimes with less of the underlying tooling. Platforms tend to cost more and cover more. Reseller programs suit agencies that already handle part of the work themselves.

### Which white label SEO tools do agencies actually use?

Most agencies rely on three things: a rank-tracking and audit tool, a reporting dashboard they can rebrand, and a link-building supply. The platforms above package those differently, so compare on the piece you cannot build in house. Our [SEO tools compared](/blog/seo-tools-compared) guide breaks the tool layer down further.

### What should I look for in the best SEO reseller?

Look at four things: how transparent the pricing is, how deep the white-label branding goes, how strong the link-building supply is, and how fast support answers when a client escalates. Support and links decide retention more than any feature list, because those are what clients notice when something goes wrong.

### Can a small agency run white label SEO without hiring?

Yes, and that is the main reason the model exists. A small agency can sell SEO on day one and let the platform do delivery, then bring functions in house as revenue grows. Start with one platform, learn its reporting, and only add a second once you know which service you want to own yourself.
