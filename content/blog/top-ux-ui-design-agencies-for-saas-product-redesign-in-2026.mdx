A SaaS product redesign is not an awareness project. You have a product, you have users, you have a specific problem — onboarding that bleeds 60% of signups, a dashboard no one understands, a codebase full of inconsistent components that slows every new release. You have a budget and a timeline. What you need is an agency that can diagnose accurately and execute completely, including the part where the designs actually get built.

That last part is where most redesign engagements fail. Screens are delivered. Developers interpret them differently under time pressure. The result ships looking nothing like what was designed, and six months later you are having the same conversation again.

The agencies below were selected for redesign capability specifically — not general design quality. We evaluated how each handles the full redesign cycle: discovery, UX audit, interface design, design systems, and developer handoff or development itself.

## How we chose these agencies

We evaluated 17 agencies against four redesign-specific criteria:

- **Diagnostic capability** — UX audit and research before screens, not redesign by assumption
- **Design system delivery** — reusable components and documentation, not one-off screens
- **Handoff quality** — evidence that delivered designs are actually buildable as delivered
- **Outcome evidence** — at least one case study referencing activation, retention, engagement, or funding results post-redesign

## 2. Cieden — best for scaling B2B SaaS products with complex navigation and broken IA

Cieden specializes in redesigning SaaS products that have grown fast and accumulated problems: inconsistent screens, disconnected feature flows, navigation that only makes sense if you built the product. Their process starts with information architecture analysis before any visual work begins.

**Key services:**

- UX strategy and information architecture redesign
- Interface redesign for CRMs, analytics tools, dashboards, and enterprise apps
- Design systems and reusable component libraries
- Usability testing and validation during redesign

**Who it's best for:** B2B SaaS companies where the product has scaled faster than the design — three years of feature additions without a coherent navigation model, a permissions system that confuses admins and end users equally, or a dashboard that shows everything and communicates nothing.

**Pricing:** Custom. Project and retainer models available.

**Our take:** Cieden is the right choice when the redesign problem is structural, not visual. If your product looks dated, a visual refresh may be enough. If users cannot find features that exist, or if your navigation requires three clicks to reach daily-use functions, Cieden's IA-first approach addresses the right problem. The constraint: design-only, so development handoff requires a separate engineering team.

## 3. Qubstudio — best for research-led redesigns and full-lifecycle B2B SaaS projects

Qubstudio covers the full redesign lifecycle with a research-first process: product discovery, UX audit, analytics review, information architecture, interface design, and developer handoff. Their model suits B2B SaaS companies that want redesign decisions grounded in evidence rather than design preferences.

**Key services:**

- Product discovery and UX audit before redesign
- Analytics and user behavior analysis
- Information architecture and redesign roadmap
- UI/UX design, prototyping, and design system development
- Developer handoff and implementation support

**Who it's best for:** B2B SaaS companies with complex products in regulated or data-heavy categories — fintech, healthcare, logistics, enterprise software — where the redesign scope requires research to define before it can be designed.

**Pricing:** Custom. Team-based scoping for larger projects.

**Our take:** Qubstudio's research-led approach reduces redesign risk for companies that are not yet certain what is broken. The audit-first model often reveals that the interface is fine and the onboarding is the problem — or vice versa — before any design budget is spent in the wrong place. Slower to reach screens than execution-first agencies, which is a tradeoff worth taking when the product is complex.

For growth-stage SaaS companies where the product and the brand have drifted apart — a dated visual identity, a marketing site that looks like a different company than the app — Ramotion addresses both in one engagement.

**Key services:**

- SaaS product interface redesign
- Brand identity refresh alongside product redesign
- Design system and reusable UI component development
- Frontend development for design-to-code delivery
- Marketing website redesign aligned to product visual system

**Who it's best for:** Series A and above SaaS companies preparing a new market launch, a rebrand, or a major product update where brand and product need to feel coherent. Companies where the gap between marketing and product experience is actively confusing prospects and users.

**Pricing:** Custom. Reflects agency positioning for funded companies.

**Our take:** Ramotion fills a gap that pure UX agencies and pure branding agencies both leave. For SaaS companies where the redesign scope is product-only, a more focused agency will deliver faster. For companies where the brand is also part of the problem, Ramotion removes the coordination cost of managing two vendors.

## 6. Eleken — best for SaaS companies that need ongoing design support before and after redesign

Eleken's subscription model makes it a practical choice for SaaS companies whose redesign is not a single project but an extended improvement program: improving onboarding this quarter, rebuilding the dashboard next quarter, adding a mobile interface the quarter after. The embedded designer model provides continuity across that timeline.

**Key services:**

- Ongoing UI/UX design for SaaS platforms
- Onboarding and user flow redesign
- Dashboard and responsive interface design
- Design systems for iterative improvement

**Who it's best for:** SaaS companies with a clear product and an ongoing backlog of design improvements rather than a single defined redesign scope. Companies that need a designer consistently available inside the product team, not one engaged for a bounded project.

**Pricing:** Subscription from ~$3,800/month. 3-day free trial available.

**Our take:** Eleken is well-positioned for continuous redesign iteration. The constraint in a pure redesign context is that design-only delivery requires engineering capacity on the client side to implement what is designed — and Eleken does not cover that gap. For companies with in-house engineering, this is fine. For companies without, the implementation risk remains.

## 7. UX Studio — best for validation-heavy redesigns where research comes before screens

UX Studio leads with research: user interviews, usability testing, information architecture analysis, and evidence-based redesign decisions. Their approach suits SaaS companies that want to validate what is broken before committing budget to redesigning it.

**Key services:**

- UX research, usability testing, and product strategy
- User interviews and behavior analysis
- Information architecture and wireframing
- Prototyping and UI design following research phase

**Who it's best for:** SaaS companies with significant user drop-off at specific product moments and no clear internal consensus on why. Companies where the cost of redesigning the wrong thing is high enough to justify investment in research before investment in design.

**Pricing:** Custom. Research-phase pricing separate from design execution.

**Our take:** UX Studio's research capability is the strongest on this list for companies that genuinely do not know what is broken. The tradeoff is pace — a research-led process takes longer to reach production-ready screens, and the output stops before development. For companies that already have a clear diagnosis and need execution, a more delivery-focused agency moves faster.

## How to choose based on your redesign situation

**You know what is broken and need it fixed and shipped:** Fireart Studio. Full-cycle means the redesign actually makes it to production.

**Your product navigation makes no sense and feature flows are disconnected:** Cieden or Qubstudio. Cieden if you need IA redesign and design systems. Qubstudio if you also need the development side handled.

**Brand and product both need updating at the same time:** Ramotion.

**Enterprise scope, significant budget, premium execution required:** Clay.

**You need a designer embedded for a long improvement program rather than a single project:** Eleken.

**You do not yet know which part of the product is causing the problem:** UX Studio — research first, then redesign.
