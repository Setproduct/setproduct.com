We invite you to [submit a guest post](https://publish.setproduct.com/) to our blog. Accelerate is $390 with one link to your site and a 24-hour turnaround. Maximize is $490 with two links, a 4-hour turnaround, homepage placement and a newsletter send. If you have no time to write, Delegate is $890: you send a short brief, our writers produce the article and the visuals, and it goes live in 7 days.
Every article goes through the same editorial review, whichever plan you pick.

We share web and graphic design ideas, inspiration tutorials as well as useful resources.

New to this? Learn [how to find guest posting opportunities](https://www.setproduct.com/blog/guest-posting-opportunities) and the full [benefits of guest blogging](https://www.setproduct.com/blog/benefits-of-guest-blogging) first.

Our blog appeals to graphic & web designers and developers, small business owners, freelancers yet those creative-minded.

## Why write for us

There are several benefits to writing for our blog:

-   Author credit for the post content.
-   Add a short bio at the end of your post.
-   Access to one of the largest audiences in design and development.
-   Being associated with a strong, established brand in the industry.

## Topics of interest to us

We accept guest post submissions on any design/development topic:

-   **Graphic design**: techniques, tutorials, tools, and inspiration.
-   **Website design and development**: guides, best practices, frameworks, coding tips, and case studies.
-   **Video design and creation**: tutorials for editing, animation, software reviews, and interesting ideas.
-   **SEO**: comprehensive strategy, tools, and recent updates.
-   **Social media and other digital marketing**: tendencies, strategies, tools, and case studies.

## Guest posting guidelines

![Checklist clipboard marking the guest post submission guidelines](/blog/assets/submit-a-guest-post/img-1.webp "float-right")

We look forward to your submissions, welcome to the blog! Please follow these guest post submission guidelines to maintain the quality of our content and provide value to our readers by submitting a guest post:

**1\. Original and Non-plagiarized Content**: Your post has to be 100% original, non-plagiarized content on our blog. Plagiarism or duplication will be rejected. 

**2\. Relevant Topics**: The content should be something that we consider relevant to our niche, such as graphic design, web design, website development, SEO, social media marketing, internet marketing, or other technology niches. 

**3\. Article Length**: Minimum 1,000 words. Up to 3,000 words on Accelerate and up to 5,000 on Maximize. No fluff, no repetition.

**4\. AI-Generated Content Policy**: We do not support obviously AI-created content. However, we are not against AI content that was rewritten and edited like human text after it has been generated as long as  it meets our quality standards.

**5\. Quality Standards**: A post should be well-written, grammatically flawless, and contain original, useful, organized helpful content. Write in an approachable, conversational style that is both entertaining and easy-to-read.

**6\. Structure with Headings**: Use headings, subheadings, and short paragraphs to improve readability. Use an uppercase letter for headings.

**7\. Links**: Up to 2 links to your company website, placed where they help the reader. Link to sources wherever you cite them. No affiliate links, shortened URLs, naked URLs or vanity URLs. Links to casinos, essays, sports betting, gambling, CBD, dating, or other inappropriate topics will not work.

**8\. Citations and Attribution**: All facts, figures, quotes, and photos must be properly attributed. You must cite your sources as proof of credibility and transparency.

**9\. Images**: Supporting images with appropriate attribution may be included to complement the content and make the post more visually appealing. However, the images should not be stock images; instead, they should be highly original or from accredited sources. A featured image for the post is necessary.

**10\. Banned Content**: Your article is not allowed to be inferred or contain references/links with dating, gambling, pornography/violence/racism, and other taboo topics. We also do not approve CBD, adult content, loans or payday, vaping, etc.

**11\. Submission Format**: Upload your draft in your dashboard after checkout as .doc, .docx, .pdf, .txt or .md, with images as separate files. Include your proposed title and meta description.

**12\. Authorship and Bio**: At the end of your post you can insert a short author bio (1-2 sentences) at the bottom with links to your own website or social media. Even include a cv with an image of the author to profile, must be present.

**13\. Editorial Rights**: We may edit the submission for clarity, structure and accuracy. You keep ownership of your article and grant us a license to publish it. We have the right to reject any post that does not adhere to our criteria, or for the reason of our choosing. Additionally, we reserve the right to eliminate outgoing links which are considered harmful for our search rankings or if its linked content changes considerably.

**14\. How to Submit**: Pick a plan on the publish page, then upload your draft in your dashboard.

We invite you to submit your guest posts and become an author on our blog!

## What we accept and what we reject

Before you write, make sure your idea fits. Here is the quick version of what gets published and what gets turned down:

**We accept:**

-   ✅ Original, non-plagiarized articles from 1,000 words.
-   ✅ On-topic pieces: graphic design, web design and development, SEO, video, and digital marketing.
-   ✅ Well-structured drafts with headings, short paragraphs, and a conversational tone.
-   ✅ Properly cited facts, figures, and quotes.
-   ✅ Original or accredited images, including a featured image.

**We reject:**

-   ❌ Thin, fluffy, or obviously unedited AI-generated content.
-   ❌ Off-niche topics or banned subjects (gambling, adult, CBD, loans, vaping, and similar).
-   ❌ Affiliate links, shortened URLs, naked URLs, or more than two links to your own site.
-   ❌ Stock photos passed off as original imagery.
-   ❌ Drafts with plagiarism, poor grammar, or missing attribution.

## How to submit a guest post

We appreciate quality content and encourage you to contribute it to our blog. Be sure to follow the guest posting instructions as outlined for submission:

**Submission Process**: Please do not email your articles; instead fill out the submit form on this link. Just submit your post through our guest post from the button below.

**Submission Guidelines**: Submit your guest post adhering to our guest post guidelines so we can accept it for sure. This also means following specified topics, meeting a word count, linking properly, and attributing correctly.

**Links to Existing Blog Posts**: If you run a resource that improves one of our existing guides, send it to hello@setproduct.com. We add resources only when they help readers of that guide.

We are excited to receive your guest post and work with you on providing the best content for our community. Begin by filling out the form, or click on that little button to submit a guest blog post.

## What to expect after you submit

Here is what happens once your pitch lands with us:

-   **Review time:** We review every draft before it goes live and may ask for changes.
-   **Edits:** We may request changes to structure, links, or tone before we accept. Small copy edits we handle ourselves.
-   **Publication:** Accelerate goes live within 24 hours of your final draft, Maximize within 4 hours, Delegate 7 days after your brief.
-   **Links:** Accelerate includes one link to your site, Maximize and Delegate include two. Every post is moderated to protect the quality of the blog.
