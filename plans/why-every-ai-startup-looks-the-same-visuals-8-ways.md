# Промпты под главу «8 ways to design an AI product that doesn't look like AI»

Раздел [`строки 159-219`](content/blog/why-every-ai-startup-looks-the-same.mdx:159) — восемь тактик ❶-❽, у каждой есть заголовок и строка `→ Try this`. Задача визуализации: читатель должен за один взгляд получить список из восьми шагов и понять суть каждого, не перечитывая текст.

Формат: вертикальный, 9:16. Причина — это чеклист-раздел, его удобно листать в сторис и на мобильном, а сама статья на телефоне читается сверху вниз. Горизонтальная картинка здесь расплющила бы восемь пунктов в нечитаемую полосу.

## Общие требования (для всех трёх)

Строго белый фон `#FFFFFF`, плоский вектор, тонкие чёрные линии около 2 px, без градиента на фоне, без тени от картинки, без текстуры бумаги и рамки. Единственный акцент — фиолетовый `#7C4DFF`, вторичный — тёплый серый и один приглушённый контрастный тон для пары «было». Типографика: современный геометрический гротеск, sentence case, без декоративных шрифтов. Все подписи резкие и без ошибок. Никакого 3D, бликов и «стеклянных» эффектов — стиль сухого редакционного рисунка. Номера ❶-❽ кириллицей не рисовать, использовать обычные арабские цифры в маленьких кружках.

## Вариант A — «The eight-step audit» (вертикальный постер-чеклист, 9:16)

Create a clean flat vector vertical poster on a pure white background (#FFFFFF), no background gradient, no shadow cast on the page, no paper texture, no outer frame. It is an eight-step checklist for an editorial article about how to make an AI product look like itself instead of looking like every other AI product.

Layout: a single tall column divided into eight evenly spaced horizontal cards, running top to bottom. Each card has a small numbered circle on the left containing an arabic numeral from one to eight, a short bold label, a one-line explanation, and a tiny visual glyph on the right that shows the action, not the decoration.

Number one, own a color. Glyph: a narrow strip of many near-black swatches with one single bright purple swatch standing apart. Label: pick a color outside the category band.

Number two, typography carries the identity. Glyph: two headline bars, one in a generic geometric sans, one in a serif with visible contrast. Label: stop using the default face.

Number three, show the output instead of the orb. Glyph: a soft abstract blob with a grey cross, beside a small document with a chart and a green check mark. Label: replace the glow with a real result.

Number four, kill the sparkle. Glyph: a four-point sparkle struck through, beside a simple custom monogram mark. Label: design your own signifier.

Number five, build a motion signature. Glyph: one curved dotted path with an arrowhead and a small easing curve drawn beside it. Label: one transition used everywhere.

Number six, borrow from an adjacent world. Glyph: four tiny flat icons in a row: a printed page, a film clapperboard, a laboratory flask, a vintage map. Label: take the grammar, not the trend.

Number seven, write like a person. Glyph: two headline bars, the top one a generic slogan in grey, the bottom one a specific promise in black. Label: a promise beats a category slogan.

Number eight, light mode as differentiation. Glyph: a small dark tile beside a warm cream tile, with a thin star on the cream one. Label: when everyone is dark, light is loud.

At the very bottom of the poster, add a slim progress bar filled to the midpoint in purple #7C4DFF, with a short caption: even two of eight changes how you read.

Typography: modern geometric sans-serif, black text, sentence case, generous spacing. Palette: black, white, light grey, warm cream and purple #7C4DFF. Aspect ratio 9:16. Keep the vertical rhythm even and the cards airy. Render every label sharply and correctly spelled.

## Вариант B — «Swap this for that» (вертикальная таблица до и после, 9:16)

Create a minimal vertical before-and-after infographic on a pure white background (#FFFFFF), flat vector style, thin black line work, no background gradient, no drop shadow, no texture, no border. It presents eight swaps for an editorial article about escaping the generic AI startup look.

Layout: a single tall column of eight stacked rows. Each row is split into two equal halves by a thin vertical rule. The left half is titled The default and drawn in pale grey and near-black. The right half is titled The alternative and drawn with the purple #7C4DFF accent. A small arrow sits on the divider pointing right. Every row keeps the identical internal structure so the eye can scan the column fast. Use the eight pairs below, each with a compact icon plus a two to four word label.

Row one: a dark background swatch versus a distinctive single color swatch. Label pair: category black and an owned color.

Row two: a generic geometric headline bar versus a serif headline bar. Label pair: default face and a face with a voice.

Row three: a glowing orb versus a real product screenshot with a document and a chart. Label pair: abstract glow and visible output.

Row four: a four-point sparkle versus a custom monogram. Label pair: borrowed glyph and your own mark.

Row five: a static frame versus the same frame repeated three times with a motion trail. Label pair: no motion language and one motion signature.

Row six: a row of identical app icons versus a page, a clapperboard, a flask and a map. Label pair: same trend and an adjacent world.

Row seven: a generic copilot slogan bar versus a specific promise bar. Label pair: category slogan and a real promise.

Row eight: a near-black tile versus a warm cream tile with a serif headline. Label pair: the default dark and light as a signal.

Add one thin footer strip at the bottom with the short sentence: pick two rows and change them this week.

Typography: modern geometric sans-serif, black, sentence case, uniform weight, no decorative type. Palette: black, white, light grey, warm cream and purple #7C4DFF. Aspect ratio 9:16, generous white space, all text sharp and correctly spelled.

## Вариант C — «The escape ladder» (вертикальная схема-лестница, 9:16)

Create a clean flat vector vertical diagram on a pure white background (#FFFFFF), thin black outlines, no background gradient, no shadow, no texture, no frame. It turns eight design moves into a ladder of rising distinctiveness for an editorial article about making an AI product look like itself.

Layout: a tall vertical ladder running from the bottom of the canvas to the top, drawn as eight evenly spaced rungs. Beside each rung, a compact card with a small number, a short label, and a tiny glyph. The cards alternate slightly left and right so the shape reads as a staircase, and a single purple #7C4DFF line traces the ascent from the lowest rung to the highest.

Rung one, bottom: own a color nobody in your category uses. Glyph: one bright swatch among many dark ones.
Rung two: make typography carry the identity. Glyph: a serif headline bar next to a generic sans bar.
Rung three: show the output instead of the orb. Glyph: a document with a chart replacing a blurred glow.
Rung four: kill the sparkle and design your own signifier. Glyph: a struck-through sparkle beside a custom mark.
Rung five: build a motion signature. Glyph: one dotted path with an easing curve.
Rung six: borrow from an adjacent physical world. Glyph: a printed page, a clapperboard, a flask and a map.
Rung seven: write like a person. Glyph: a specific promise bar replacing a slogan bar.
Rung eight, top: light mode as differentiation. Glyph: a warm cream tile with a serif headline and a thin star.

Along the left edge, draw a thin vertical axis labelled how recognizable you look, with three small tick marks and no numbers. At the top of the ladder place a short sentence case caption: recognizable beats interchangeable.

Typography: modern geometric sans-serif, black, sentence case, no shouting weight except the top caption. Palette: black, white, light grey, warm cream and purple #7C4DFF. Aspect ratio 9:16, airy and legible at thumbnail size, all text sharply rendered and correctly spelled.
