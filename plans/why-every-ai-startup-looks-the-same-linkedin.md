## Версия A — «Двести продуктов, ни одного в памяти» (диагностика)

Крючок — прямо из интро статьи. Работает на боли узнавания: читатель вспоминает свой последний Demo Day.

```text
Two hundred AI products. Logos taped over. I could not name a single one.

Scroll a YC Demo Day list, then do the same on Product Hunt's AI tab. Every card is a near-black rectangle, a purple glow in one corner, a sparkle icon, and a sentence about a copilot.

Strip the names and the whole list collapses into one company repeated two hundred times.

That is not a style. It is a uniform. And a uniform makes you invisible.

The uncomfortable part:

Lindgaard measured it years ago. People form a visual judgment in about 50 milliseconds, and it stays stable on repeat exposure. If your page carries no distinguishing mark, there is nothing to store. You paid for the traffic and bought zero recall.

There is a second cost nobody talks about. When a buyer cannot see a difference between two tools, the only axis left to compare is the price. Design is the strongest non-price signal a small company has. The uniform throws it away.

I wrote the full teardown: the anatomy of the AI landing page in 2026, the five forces that produced it, five companies that broke out, and eight tactics that work.

Break one or two rules and you are already more recognizable than most.

https://www.setproduct.com/blog/why-every-ai-startup-looks-the-same
```

## Версия B — «Шесть ответов “да” — вы в форме» (чеклист, максимальная вовлечённость)

Крючок — интерактивный. Ведёт к чеклисту из 10 пунктов, провоцирует комментарии-самооценки.

```text
Six "yes" answers and you are wearing the uniform.

Score your own landing page honestly. Yes or no:

1. Hero background within a few shades of #0A0A0A?
2. Gradient CTA on a dark background?
3. Purple or blue radial glow behind the headline?
4. The four-point sparkle icon anywhere in the product?
5. H1 built on the word copilot or agent?
6. Inter or Geist as your only typeface?
7. Bento grid of rounded tiles in the features section?
8. Logo marquee of "trusted by" companies above the fold?
9. A name ending in -ly or -ify, or containing "AI"?
10. A chat input box in the hero instead of a stated promise?

Six or more: you are invisible in a feed of ten screenshots.
Four or five: you are on the edge.
Three or fewer: you have something worth protecting.

None of these is a crime on its own. Stacked together they become a fingerprint, and every competitor has the same one.

The fix is not a rebrand. It is one or two decisions: own a color nobody in your category uses, replace the glow with a real product output, write the H1 as a specific promise instead of a category slogan.

Full checklist plus eight tactics, in order of impact:

https://www.setproduct.com/blog/why-every-ai-startup-looks-the-same

How many did you score? Genuinely curious.
```

## Версия C — «Самый тёмный интерфейс достался самым тонким продуктам» (мнение + кейсы)

Крючок — парадокс. Ведёт к главам про wrapper suspicion и разбор Anthropic, Perplexity, ElevenLabs, Midjourney, Runway.

```text
The darkest look in software now belongs to the thinnest products.

That sentence should worry every founder who ships an AI landing page.

Thousands of GPT wrappers shipped on #0A0A0A backgrounds with a sparkle and a chat box. The visual became a tell. If your product has real depth and you dress it in wrapper clothing, you invite the exact skepticism you spent months trying to avoid.

Buyers judge the costume long before they look at the engine.

What did the ones who escaped do differently? They stopped describing their technology and started describing their outcome.

- Anthropic went warm where the market went black: off-white, a serif voice, terracotta. The palette and the safety-first values point the same direction.
- Perplexity looks like a newsroom, not a chatbot. For a product selling trust in answers, that does more work than any feature list.
- ElevenLabs stripped color to a minimum and kept a single waveform. Restraint became the identity.
- Midjourney barely has a UI. It shows output, not dashboard chrome.

The move is the same in all five: declare the world you belong to, and stop declaring the mechanism you happen to use.

Five teardowns and eight tactics in the full piece:

https://www.setproduct.com/blog/why-every-ai-startup-looks-the-same
```
